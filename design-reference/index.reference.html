Would You Rather Questions for Kids

Fun and age-appropriate questions for children, classrooms, birthday parties, family game nights and long car rides.

  1. Would you rather have a pet dragon or have a pet unicorn?

  2. Would you rather be able to fly or be able to turn invisible?

  3. Would you rather eat pizza every day or eat ice cream every day?

  4. Would you rather live in a giant treehouse or live in an underwater house?

  5. Would you rather explore outer space or explore the deepest ocean?

  6. Would you rather speak every language or talk to animals?

Would You Rather Questions for Adults

Awkward, difficult and slightly spicy questions for adult parties, date nights, couples and groups of friends.

  1. Would you rather have your search history made public or have your camera roll made public?

  2. Would you rather text your ex right now or let your friend read your last 20 messages?

  3. Would you rather always know when someone is lying or always get away with lying?

  4. Would you rather reveal your most embarrassing moment or hear everyone’s honest opinion of you?

  5. Would you rather be famous but hated or be unknown but deeply loved?

  6. Would you rather never be able to lie or never be believed?

How to Play Would You Rather

No downloads, accounts, equipment or complicated rules are required.

1

Read the choices

Everyone gets the same two funny, strange or difficult options.

2

Pick one answer

You must choose option A or option B. “Neither” is not allowed.

3

Explain your choice

The best conversations happen when everyone explains their answer.
